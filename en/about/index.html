From the workshop to leadership

Sylvain Brochu learned his trade with his hands in the material. That is what gives him credibility today when he leads craftspeople.

After studying fashion marketing at Académie Julien, he joined Cirque du Soleil headquarters in 1999, cutting, preparing and sewing costumes.

In 2003, he went on international tour with Varekai. Over four years, he learned how to keep a production running on the road: costumes, suppliers, shipping, pallets and ATA carnets. He also designed costumes for the LOVE premiere in Las Vegas and the Corteo premiere in New York, then for events in Rome and Turkey.

In 2007, he founded Even Even Design, une entreprise de luminaires sur mesure et de pièces uniques. Avec un réseau de soudeurs, de découpeurs CNC, de souffleurs de verre et de monteurs, il équipe large private residences and events for Cirque du Soleil's events division. He also founded Onomiko, a brand of luxury robes, tunics and wrap dresses, presented in three fashion shows.

From 2015 to 2024, he ran operations at the Never Apart centre for the Age of Union foundation: more than 35 exhibitions, up to 10 at a time, a team of employees and installers, and the entire building.

In 2026, he was part of the team of props makers for the Formula 1 Grand Prix show.

Now a Project Manager at Jack World, he continues to create his own custom light fixtures, costumes and Onomiko pieces, while sharing his craft with the next generation of makers.

How he works

Four traits that show up in all his projects.

He knows the trades

Sewing, carpentry, welding, glass, finishing: he has practised or managed each of these trades. He speaks the craftspeople's language and knows what a schedule asks of them.

He does a lot with a little

At the Never Apart centre, budgets were tight. He learned to negotiate every purchase and to choose the option that gets the best result for the least money.

He plans for the road

Four years of touring taught him to plan packing, transport and teardown from the design stage.

He wants to pass it on

Creating, leading teams in the field and passing on to the next generation of craftspeople what twenty-five years in workshops and on stage have taught him.

A custom piece or a project?

Custom light fixtures, Onomiko pieces, costumes, scenography or collaboration: reach out directly to Sylvain.
