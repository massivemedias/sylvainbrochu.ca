Driftwood

Treated or dipped driftwood, assembled into lamps, pendants and custom chandeliers.

Type
Lamps and pendants
Materials
Driftwood, grapevine, jute
Models
Bocage, Bora, Drift
Company
Even Even Design

The models

  • Bocage: driftwood base with a 14-inch woven shade in grey or white.
  • Bora: dipped driftwood, a grapevine ball and jute-knit covered cord.
  • Drift: a driftwood branch pendant.
  • Custom pendants and chandeliers in treated driftwood.
