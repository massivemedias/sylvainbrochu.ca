Blown glass

Bubbles blown by glass artisans, hung from custom steel structures.

Type
Pendants
Materials
Blown glass, steel, bamboo
Made with
Glass artisans
Company
Even Even Design

The models

  • Crackle-glass bubbles with a bamboo or perforated steel cap.
  • Asymée: an asymmetric blown-glass bubble.
  • Asymée Barika: the giant version.
  • Steel hooks and structures to hang the bubbles.

Directing a glassblower

Every bubble that leaves the glassblower's studio is unique. His job: give the artisans clear direction, then design the steel structure that holds the bubbles.
