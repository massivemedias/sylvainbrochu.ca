Private residences

Light fixtures drawn for one house and for the people who live in it.

Clients
Private clients
Location
Saint-Hyacinthe, among others
Company
Even Even Design
Role
Design, fabrication, installation

Two large residences in Saint-Hyacinthe

For each one, months of fabrication with his network of craftspeople, then a week of installation on site. Every fixture is custom-designed for the room it lights.

Private commissions

Blown-glass bubbles on a steel ceiling rose, wall sconces, pendants: other clients have trusted him with one-of-a-kind pieces for their homes.

His role

  • Designing the pieces with the client.
  • Coordinating fabrication with welders, glassblowers and craftspeople.
  • Delivery and installation.
