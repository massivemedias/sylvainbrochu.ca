Even Even collection

Since 2007, Even Even Design has created clean-lined light fixtures inspired by raw materials and its founder's travels.

Company
Even Even Design
Founded
2007
Location
Montreal
Role
Founder and designer

The pieces

  • Vaporis: cut-metal pendant.
  • Tricopod: jute-knit covered tripod with a custom shade.
  • Steel pendants with filament bulbs.
  • Aluminum dome lamp.
  • Emodulia: a light installation of hanging tubes.

Salon des designers québécois
