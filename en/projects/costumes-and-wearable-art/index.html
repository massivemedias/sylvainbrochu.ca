Costumes and wearable art

He started his career cutting and sewing costumes. At Never Apart he kept designing them: feathers for Pride, ruffles for a photo campaign, wearable art for Wiggle.

Organization
Never Apart, Age of Union
Years
2015-2021
Locations
Place Émilie-Gamelin, Montreal Museum of Fine Arts, Never Apart centre
Role
Costume and prop design

Village Paradise, Montreal Pride 2015

On August 14, 2015, Never Apart and Fierté Montreal produced the Village Paradise show at Place Émilie-Gamelin. Sylvain created the Birds of Paradise feather costumes, worn on stage by Plastik Patrik, Mikela Jay and Jordan King.

Rose Tint, summer 2021 campaign

For Never Apart's summer 2021 visual campaign, he made the costume and props: a plunging sequinned gown, pink ruffled wings and a chain headpiece. Art direction by Fritz Picault, photography by Damian Siqueiros, with model Leila Rugmbarara.

Wiggle at the Montreal Museum of Fine Arts, 2019

On May 1, 2019, the Wiggle wearable art show moved into the Montreal Museum of Fine Arts, alongside its Thierry Mugler exhibition. Sylvain is listed among the show's wearable art designers, next to The House of Venus and Never Apart.

And now, Onomiko

That know-how carries on in Onomiko, his label of robes, tunics and wrap dresses.
