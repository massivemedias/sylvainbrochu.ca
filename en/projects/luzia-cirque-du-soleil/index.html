Luzia, Cirque du Soleil

In 2016, he returned to Cirque du Soleil to build stage props for Luzia, working with the show's production teams.

Client
Cirque du Soleil
Show
Luzia
Year
2016
Role
Prop design and fabrication

The pieces

  • The horse: head, neck, mane and articulated legs.
  • A puppet operated by three people.
  • The marimbas: fabrication, cladding and decoration.
  • Banquet chairs modified and reinforced for the stage.
  • Finishing work on a trampoline table.

Building for a touring show

A stage prop has to look good from a distance, hold up close and stay safe for the performers who handle it night after night. After four years on tour with Varekai, he knows those demands from the inside.
