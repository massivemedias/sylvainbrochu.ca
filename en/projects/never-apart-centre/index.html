Never Apart Centre

Nine years keeping an art centre running: more than 35 exhibitions, up to 10 at a time across a dozen rooms.

Organization
Age of Union Foundation
Location
Montreal
Years
2015-2024
Role
Operations manager and production planner

The brief

Plan and deliver about four exhibition seasons a year, with around ten artists at a time, and run the building's operations.

His role

  • Planning and coordinating more than 35 exhibitions and cultural projects.
  • Up to 10 simultaneous exhibitions across a dozen rooms.
  • Leading 2 to 3 part-time employees depending on the season, and 2 to 3 assistant installers depending on the seasonal set-ups, plus craftspeople and subcontractors.
  • Budgets, purchasing and supplier negotiations.
  • Building management and maintenance.

Doing a lot with a little

Production budgets were tight. He learned to get the most out of them: negotiating every purchase, choosing the right suppliers and building things himself when that was the best option, like the centre's staircase railing.

Openings, Wiggle, Equinox and Solstice

Beyond the exhibitions, he provided technical and logistical support for everything happening at the centre: each season's openings, the Wiggle wearable art show and the Equinox and Solstice music nights, which draw thousands of people. He also made costumes for the centre's shows and photo campaigns.

The centre in numbers

Located in Mile-Ex, the centre has 12,000 square feet of galleries, a music production studio, a cinema, an outdoor pool and an archive of 10,000 records. Between its opening in June 2015 and the summer of 2021, it welcomed more than 83,000 visitors, hosted 1,350 artist collaborations and held 575 events.
