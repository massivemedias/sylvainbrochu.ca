Baricaut

Cedar slats held by a steel cage, made in sizes from small up to 60 inches in diameter.

Type
Signature light fixture
Materials
Cedar and steel
Sizes
Small up to 60 inches
Company
Even Even Design

One piece, many scales

The Baricaut comes as a pendant or a floor lamp, in a small size or a giant version. Filament bulbs light the cedar slats from the inside.

Made to measure

Every Baricaut can be custom-made for the space. It hangs above dining tables and boardroom tables alike.
