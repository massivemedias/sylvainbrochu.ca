Cirque du Soleil

Eight years at Cirque du Soleil, from the Montreal headquarters to the Varekai international tour.

Years
1999-2007, then 2016
Locations
Montreal and international tour
Shows
Varekai, LOVE, Corteo, Luzia
Role
Costumes, fabrication and production

Headquarters, 1999-2003

Cutting, preparing and sewing costumes.

Varekai tour, 2003-2007

Four years on the road: preparing and maintaining costumes, coordinating needs and adapting to the constraints of touring. He also set up the backstage dressing areas: wigs, shoes, sewing supplies and portable machines.

International logistics

For international events: coordinating with suppliers, tracking schedules, preparing shipments, packing, pallets and ATA carnets.

Premieres and events

  • Corteo premiere, New York, 2006: costume designer for the evening's acrobatic entertainment, about 50 performers. Art direction by Michel Laprise.
  • LOVE premiere, Las Vegas, 2006: assistant costume designer for the late-night entertainment at The Mirage hotel, more than 350 performers, acrobats and extras. Art direction by Michel Laprise.
  • Fiat Bravo launch, Rome, 2007: assistant costume designer for five shows. Art direction by Danielle Labrie.
  • Opening of the FIBA World Championship, Turkey, 2010: costume designer with the events division. Art direction by Michel Laprise.

Back in 2016

He returned to Cirque du Soleil to build stage props for Luzia.
