Maison Grinder

Some 100 custom light fixtures, from the front desk to the 16 guest rooms of a boutique hotel in Griffintown.

Client
Maison Grinder, boutique hotel
Location
Griffintown, Montreal
Company
Even Even Design
Role
Design, installation, art direction

The brief

Fit out an entire boutique hotel with custom-made light fixtures: the lobby and front desk, the hallways, the 16 guest rooms, the terrace and the restaurant.

His role

  • Designing the light fixtures for every space in the hotel.
  • Coordinating his network of craftspeople and subcontractors for fabrication.
  • Installing all the fixtures on site.
  • Art directing the installation, space by space.

Run like a production

Around a hundred pieces to fabricate, subcontractors to coordinate and an installation to plan in every space of the hotel: the kind of project where his production experience makes the difference.
