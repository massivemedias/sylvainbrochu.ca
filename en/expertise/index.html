Expertise

Twenty-five years of workshop, touring and operations work have given him a complete view of a production, from the first sketch to teardown.

Production management

Planning, budgeting, coordinating and delivering. At the Never Apart centre, he managed up to 10 exhibitions at once, four seasons a year.

  • Planning and scheduling
  • Budgets, purchasing and cost control
  • Coordinating teams, artists and suppliers
  • Delivering on schedule and to standard
Examples: Never Apart Centre Formula 1 Grand Prix

Fabrication and subcontracting

He started out as a craftsman. He knows what a schedule asks of a welder, a glassblower or an installer, and he leads them accordingly.

  • A network of welders, CNC cutters, glassblowers, printers, graphic designers and installers
  • Prototyping and material selection
  • Quality control, from the workshop to installation
  • Negotiating prices and assembly
Examples: Maison Grinder Baricaut Blown glass

Logistics and touring

Four years on international tour with Varekai taught him how to move a production.

  • Shipping, packing and pallets
  • ATA carnets and backstage technical equipment
  • Coordinating suppliers abroad
  • Premieres in Las Vegas, New York, Rome and Turkey
Examples: Cirque du Soleil

Scenography, sets and lighting

Stage props, sets and ambient lighting for live shows, events and hospitality.

  • Scenic props and puppets
  • Large-scale custom light fixtures
  • Event decor and lighting
  • Art direction of installations
Examples: Luzia, Cirque du Soleil Events Maison Grinder

Operations and facilities

Keeping a venue ready to produce: rooms, equipment, maintenance and staff.

  • Operations management for an exhibition centre
  • Maintenance and technical work
  • Supervising employees and installers
Examples: Never Apart Centre
