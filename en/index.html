From first idea to opening night

How he runs a production, from the first budget to the last truck.

  1. 1

    Plan

    Budget, schedule and purchasing, negotiated before fabrication begins.

  2. 2

    Assemble

    The right welder, the right glassblower, the right installer. A network built one project at a time.

  3. 3

    Lead fabrication

    Research, prototypes, workshop follow-up. He knows the trades he manages.

  4. 4

    Deliver and install

    Transport, setup and art direction of the installation on site.

  5. 5

    Strike and move on

    Teardown the same night, then packing, pallets and shipping for the next date.
