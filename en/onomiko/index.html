A label by Sylvain Brochu

Onomiko

Robes, tunics and wrap dresses, designed and made in Montreal. Roomy cuts inspired by traditional garments from around the world, in printed satins, voiles and lamés.

The collectionOnomiko shop

Onomiko is what you slip on to host, to go out or to walk on stage. Sylvain designs every piece and follows its production from the workshop to the runway.

The launch

September 13, 2025

The collection was presented on September 13, 2025, at the Onomiko x VRSTL Records launch night, with the Montreal record label.

On the bill: a runway show in front of a wall of hanging gowns, dance, acrobatics and singing, and a room packed up to the balcony.

From the rack to the runway

Before the show, Sylvain dresses the models himself and checks every piece at the rack, list in hand.

The designer

Sylvain Brochu

Sylvain Brochu founded Onomiko. He designs the pieces and manages production, orders and sales to private clients. He has created and produced three runway shows for the label in three years.

The label continues a career that began in clothing: fashion marketing studies at Académie Julien, cutting and sewing costumes at Cirque du Soleil, costumes for the Corteo premiere in New York and the LOVE premiere in Las Vegas, then costumes and props for Never Apart.
